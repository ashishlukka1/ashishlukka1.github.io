## overview

a focused learning space for college students. sign in with clerk and work through curated MERN stack lectures, organized into modules, all in one place.

## highlights

- **simple sign-in:** clerk authentication for student accounts.
- **structured path:** curated lectures grouped into modules, so there is always a clear next step.
- **focused viewing:** a video playback interface built for learning without distractions.
- **pick up where you left off:** browse and continue a structured learning path.
