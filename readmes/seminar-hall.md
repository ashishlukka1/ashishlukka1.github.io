## overview

a web app for booking seminar halls at VNRVJIET, replacing manual requests with online reservations that admins approve.

## highlights

- **easy reservations:** pick a hall, date and time slot online.
- **club registration:** campus organizations can register through the app.
- **admin approvals:** admins approve hall bookings and club requests.
- **calendar view:** see all bookings in one calendar, filtered by hall.
- **self-service:** users manage their own bookings and account settings.
