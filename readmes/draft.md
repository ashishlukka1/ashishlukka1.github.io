## overview

a full-stack MERN publishing platform where readers, authors and admins each get their own workflow. clerk handles authentication, and a rich text editor makes writing and editing articles simple.

## highlights

- **role-based flows:** separate experiences for users, authors and admins, with role selection during signup.
- **secure sign-in:** clerk authentication.
- **rich text editing:** create and edit articles in a full editor.
- **find and discuss:** search and filter articles, and join the conversation through comments.
- **moderation:** admins can block users and moderate content.
