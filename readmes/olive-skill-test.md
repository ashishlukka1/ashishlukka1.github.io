## overview

an internal training and assessment platform built during my internship at olive crypto systems. it takes a team member from course to certificate, with separate dashboards for employees, checkers and admins.

## the flow

1. a course is built from units and reviewed before it is published.
2. employees work through the units, and each unit ends with a randomized assessment.
3. progress is tracked and assignments are reviewed for every employee.
4. a certificate is generated automatically once all units and assessments are complete.

## highlights

- **three dashboards:** tailored views for employees, checkers and admins.
- **course management:** create courses and units, with a review step before publishing.
- **fair assessments:** randomized questions for each unit, with tab-switch detection during tests.
- **progress at a glance:** track each employee and review their assignments.
- **automatic certificates:** no manual paperwork once someone finishes.
